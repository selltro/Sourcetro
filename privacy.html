SourceTro — From Source to Sold.

Draft Privacy Policy

Draft Version 0.1 · Last updated August 6, 2026

This draft explains, in general terms, how SourceTro may handle information when helping resellers create, organize, and prepare product listings. The final policy must be updated to match the services, vendors, storage systems, and marketplace permissions actually used at public launch.

Information SourceTro may process

  • Name, email address, account details, and support messages.
  • Product photos, typed notes, voice-to-text transcripts, listing drafts, measurements, item costs, pricing information, shipping estimates, and profit calculations.
  • Marketplace account identifiers and information the user authorizes SourceTro to access.
  • Basic browser, device, diagnostic, security, and usage information.
  • Subscription and payment status when paid plans are offered. Full payment-card details should be handled by a payment processor rather than stored by SourceTro.

How information may be used

SourceTro may use information to provide requested listing assistance, save and organize drafts, calculate estimates, connect authorized marketplace features, maintain security, troubleshoot errors, improve the service as disclosed, communicate about accounts, and comply with legal obligations.

Photos, voice, and drafts

Voice may be converted into text by the user’s browser, device, or a speech-processing provider. SourceTro should not claim that raw audio is stored unless the live service actually stores it. Product photos and listing details may be sent to an AI provider when the user requests AI assistance.

The current prototype stores much of its listing and sourcing information in the browser. Information stored only in browser storage can be lost when browser data is cleared or when a different device or browser is used.

eBay and other marketplace connections

Future marketplace connections will require the user’s permission. SourceTro will request access only for enabled features and will not ask users to give SourceTro their eBay password directly. Marketplace access may be disconnected by the user when that control is available.

Sharing

Information may be shared with service providers needed to operate SourceTro, with connected marketplaces at the user’s direction, and when reasonably necessary for legal, security, or safety purposes. SourceTro does not state that personal information is sold. The final policy must identify the actual service providers and confirm any advertising practices before launch.

Data retention and security

Final retention periods for account data, photos, listing drafts, voice transcripts, marketplace tokens, logs, and billing records have not yet been established. SourceTro intends to use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards, but no internet service can guarantee complete security.

User choices

Users should be able to edit or delete drafts and photos, disconnect marketplaces, control marketing messages, and request access, correction, export, or deletion of their information. Some of these controls are still under development.

Age

SourceTro is intended for people age 18 or older and is not designed for children.

Changes and contact

Material changes to the final privacy policy should be communicated clearly. A final effective date, support email, business address, service-provider list, and retention schedule must be added before public launch.

This draft should be reviewed by a qualified attorney before public launch.
