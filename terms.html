Users must be at least 18 years old and legally able to enter agreements. Users are responsible for providing accurate account information, protecting login credentials, and reporting suspected unauthorized access.

What SourceTro does

SourceTro may help users prepare resale listing drafts using photos, typed or spoken notes, measurements, pricing estimates, shipping suggestions, profit calculations, saved drafts, and future marketplace connections.

AI-generated drafts

Tro’s suggestions may contain mistakes. SourceTro does not guarantee product identification, authenticity, condition, measurements, category, item specifics, pricing, demand, shipping cost, tax treatment, profit, marketplace approval, sales, or uninterrupted service.

The seller must review and approve every listing detail before saving or publishing.

Marketplace connections

Seller responsibilities

  • Sell only items the user lawfully owns or is authorized to sell.

  • Follow applicable laws and marketplace policies.
